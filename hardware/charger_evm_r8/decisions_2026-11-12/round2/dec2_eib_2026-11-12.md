# EIB decisions after the tap reduction (2026-11-12)

## 1. Scope

This record lists the operator decisions of 2026-11-12 on the EIB (design-state group 4). The decision session left no
separate note file; this record is transcribed from its report.

## 2. Decisions

1. **Fab status.** The EIB has not been ordered. No bare EIBs exist. The package `fab/eib_2026-11-11` needs operator
   review before an order.
2. **Sense branch.** The sense parts stay as DNP in place. The copper is unchanged (ERC 0, DRC 0 and parity stand); only
   `eib-bom.csv` and `eib-pos.csv` are regenerated with DNP flags. Accepted consequence: with R263 not fitted, an open
   R272 together with an open R272b leaves /CE floating (SLUSDV2C requires that /CE does not float). The fault is latent
   and only T-C-05 metering finds it.
3. **U204 build-time check.** U204 is not fitted. Fitting it would also fit R262 and R263; R263 then pulls /CE toward
   0 V (charge allowed) in the double-open fault, and nothing reads U204 in service. U204 also conflicts with
   J101.13 = GND (about 7 mA into R264). T-C-05 metering of R272 and R272b is the build check.
4. **J101 and W5.** J101 stays 2 x 7 with pins 11 and 13 not connected. The 2 x 5 shrink was rejected (new cable, GND
   conductors 4 to 2, re-derivation of the A.3 keying and row-shift proof).
5. **Fallback (iii).** Fallback (iii) is reopened as a TODO. It failed only on E4 and is no longer blocked, pending a
   lead-length re-check of E1, E2, E3, E5 and E7. It runs only if MS2 forces a fallback.

## 5. Findings for the design document

- Pin 9 (GND) is adjacent to pin 10 (RFC_OFF). A 9-10 short drives RFC_OFF through R132 (100 ohm) into GND at about
  33 mA, above the LVC recommended IOL. Row B.6 (about line 1847) calls the 9-10 short safe without this current. Pin 11
  NC avoids a second such pair.
