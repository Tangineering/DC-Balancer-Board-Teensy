# Footprint land-pattern check (2026-11-17)

This check compares every footprint of the CR and EIB libraries with the maker's recommended land pattern, or,
where the maker gives none, with the package drawing and the IPC-7351 nominal land. It covers pad size and
position, pitch, pad numbering against pin 1, exposed-pad and paste coverage, hole size against the lead, and
courtyards. An independent verifier tried to refute each finding. No S1 or S2 finding survived: every footprint
accepts its part with the right pin mapping. The confirmed S3 findings are mostly KiCad IPC generator lands that
differ from a maker land by tenths of a millimetre, and documentation items.

Severity: S1 = the part cannot be fitted or the pins map wrongly; S2 = a deviation that risks bridging,
tombstoning or weak joints; S3 = cosmetic or documentation.
