# BOM, schematic and datasheet check (2026-11-17)

This check compares the BOM set `bom_2026-11-14/` with the CR and EIB schematics, and the pinout and connections
of every active part and connector with its datasheet. Seven agents covered the scopes of Table 1. An independent
verifier then tried to refute each finding against the datasheet, the schematic, the BOM and the design document.
The check found no S1 or S2 defect: no wrong connection, no wrong pinout, no wrong part and no wrong rating.
Every confirmed finding is S3 (documentation or consistency). The PM9 order line of section 2 is the only finding
that changes what is ordered.

Severity: S1 = a wrong connection or part that would malfunction or cause damage; S2 = a wrong BOM line, quantity,
rating or footprint with a workaround; S3 = documentation or consistency.
