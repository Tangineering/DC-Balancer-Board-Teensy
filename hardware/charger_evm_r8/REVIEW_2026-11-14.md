# Charger EVM r8: overarching review of the CR and EIB (2026-11-14)

This document records the findings of a read-only review of the carrier board (CR) and the EVM interface board (EIB)
at commit `014c745` (D-69). It changes no design file, BOM, firmware or record; every recommended fix waits for an
operator ruling.

## 1. Scope, method and result

### 1.1 Scope

The review covered the following:

- the connections between the CR, the motherboard (MB), the BQ25798EVM and the EIB, including J-TAP;
- the component selections against their datasheets, and the connections between the components;
- the placement, routing, outlines and fab packages of both boards (`fab/cr_2026-11-13f`, `fab/eib_2026-11-13c`);
- the BOMs and order files in `bom_2026-11-13/`;
- the design documentation (`README.md`, `HANDOFF.md`, `LAYOUT_DECISIONS.md`, `RESPIN_HANDOFF_2026-11-13.md`, the
  wiring design document and its companion);
- the firmware handoff material (wiring design sections B.9, D.2-D.5, F, G and I, and
  `decisions_2026-11-12/round2/dec2_firmware_rules_2026-11-12.md`).

### 1.2 Method

Nine reviewers each took one area and checked it against the primary sources: the generators, the KiCad netlists and
boards, the MB Eagle files, the Teensy IO CSV 2026-11-12, SLUSDV2C, SLUUCB5E, the TCKE1401NM datasheet and the
manufacturer datasheets fetched from the web. An independent verifier then tried to refute each reviewer's findings
and recomputed the numbers. A completeness critic listed the scope that nobody covered (section 6). A separate agent
verified the four items that the V_rgn investigation session relayed (section 4).

### 1.3 Result

The review produced 88 verified findings: 0 at S1, 17 at S2 and 71 at S3. The verifiers rated 71 CONFIRMED and
17 PARTLY, and refuted none. Several findings describe the same defect from two areas; section 3 cross-references them.
The V_rgn verification adds four items (section 4); one of them is S1 for the board as it is now built.

Severity scale: S1 would damage hardware, make a board non-functional or unsafe, or block assembly or the order;
S2 is a wrong value, connection or document that should be fixed before ordering or bring-up but has a workaround;
S3 is a minor inconsistency, a documentation defect or polish.

No finding shows a wrong net, a wrong pin map between the boards, or a wrong datasheet pinout. Section 5 lists what
the reviewers checked and found correct, which includes the J1 22-pin mapping onto the MB, the J-TAP far-end points,
the EVM landing points, the I2C pull-up arrangement, the signal polarities across the boards and the ground scheme.

## 2. Items for the operator

### 2.1 Decisions that affect the order (copper, silkscreen, stencil or BOM)

1. **W5 wire-pad grids (D-66): GND and 3V3 pads next to the 100 ohm drivers.** INT-0, EIB-0, EIB-1. On the CR J101
   grid, GND pads 3 and 9 sit next to ILIM_REL_A (pad 7) and CE_DRV_J (pad 5), both driven through 100 ohm (R131A,
   R130). One solder bridge pulls the CR logic rail down to 1.83-2.30 V, the failure that ruling R2 (r9-12) removed.
   On both grids 3V3 sits next to CE_DRV_J, so one bridge forces /CE LOW. The charger stays in a safe direction, but
   the comparators run below the analysed rail. Options: reorder the grids (copper), raise R130/R131A into the
   3.16-10.5 kOhm window of H.24(d) (BOM), or keep the copper and add a post-solder isolation check to T-A-06/T-C-05.
2. **Wire-pad and IC orientation silkscreen.** INT-2, EIL-1, EIL-2, CRL-3. The J101 and J201 pin numbers are on F.Fab
   only, and the two 3x3 grids use different orders, so the nine W5 wires are matched from a table. U201, U202 and the
   CR ICs and diodes have no pin-1 or polarity mark on the silkscreen. Option: add pin numbers or net names and pin-1
   marks to the silkscreen before the order (no copper change).
3. **EIB J201 pads 0.153 mm from the AGND pours.** EIL-0. The D-68 0.5 mm rule covers only the E pads. A bridge from
   GND_J to the pour shorts R273, the single-point ground link. Option: extend the D-68 rule to J201, refill and
   re-export.
4. **EIB stencil prints paste on 32 pads of DNP parts.** EIB-7, EIL-6, BOM-2. The stencil row in `eib_bom.csv` is also
   mislabelled "CR top side" and cites the U101 pitch (BOM-1, a D-69 generator defect). Option: remove the DNP pads from
   F.Paste before the stencil order, or tape them off at assembly.
5. **D103 versus the U101 OUT absolute minimum.** PWR-0. The STPS2H100A does not support the recorded -0.5 to -1 V
   hard-short OUT excursion against the TCKE1401NM -0.3 V absolute minimum, and the acceptance (J.1 item 5) still
   rests on TI guidance for the replaced TPS26630. There is one U101 and no spare. Options: choose a lower-VF Schottky,
   or run the T-C-04 hard-short step last.
6. **Digi-Key cart.** BOM-0. The uploaded 13b list still holds the three pre-D-67 resistors and the 2147561101 cable;
   README 4.3 tells the operator to delete only the resistors. Option: build a fresh Digi-Key list from the current
   `digikey_import.csv`.

### 2.2 Decisions that affect bring-up, firmware or documents (no order impact)

1. **CLAUDE.md contradicts the new firmware rules.** FW-0. CLAUDE.md section 2 requires REGEN LOW before FC_CHARGE HIGH;
   FR-06 and FR-49 require the opposite, and design section D.5 says CLAUDE.md needs no change beyond D.4. The firmware
   round reads CLAUDE.md as binding.
2. **BQ25798 ADC channel masks are not specified.** FW-1. REG2F/REG30 have no image, so the 12 ms IBAT/IBUS cadence
   cannot be met with the default 66 ms scan, and the FR-48(a) one-shot VBAT read can read a stale value.
3. **The D-67 recalculation run is outstanding.** DOC-0, PWR-1. 34 `TODO(recalc: D-67)` markers remain; FR-12(b1), the
   T10 proof (b) threshold and T-B-23 still use TPS26630 figures.
4. **J-TAP cavity map.** INT-1, DOC-3. B.8 and D.1 PM3 still give the superseded r9-4 map. A harness built from them
   (the cable is now workshop stock) swaps T1 with T2 and CHG_IIN with a GND cavity.
5. **Records that point to superseded files.** DOC-1, DOC-2, DOC-4. Section E of the design document, the README
   pointers and the CR removal procedure do not reflect the 2026-11-13 rulings and D-66.
6. **The V_rgn items of section 4**, chiefly the invalid 21.2-21.5 V premise, the U101 OVP behaviour on a hard brake,
   and the FR-12(a2) threshold.

#### INT-0 (S2, PARTLY): CR J101 wire-pad grid puts the 100-ohm-driven CE_DRV_J (pad 5) and ILIM_REL_A (pad 7) next to GND pads, which brings back the bridge case that r9-12 fixed

- **Same defect as:** EIB-0, EIB-1
- **Location:** hardware/charger_evm_r8/cr/cr.kicad_pcb J101 (D-66 grid); gen/netlist.py lines for R130/R131A/R131B/R132; LAYOUT_DECISIONS.md D-66 Table D-66
- **Evidence:** These are the J101 pad centres I read from cr.kicad_pcb (KiCad frame): 3 GND at (40.60,50.63), 9 GND at (38.06,55.71), 5 CE_DRV_J at (38.06,53.17), 7 ILIM_REL_A at (40.60,53.17), 1 J3V3_1 at (38.06,50.63) and 2 J3V3_2 at (43.14,50.63). The pads are 1.8 mm on a 2.54 mm pitch, so neighbouring pads have 0.74 mm of copper gap. That puts pad 5 orthogonally next to GND pad 9, pad 7 next to GND pad 3, and pads 1 and 2 next to GND pad 3. R130 (CE_DRV) and R131A (ILIM_REL_A) are 100 ohm (netlist.py). The r9-12 change (netlist.py comment at R131B/R132; design doc H.24(d)) raised the GND-adjacent ribbon drivers to 4.99 k so that the CR 3V3 rail stays >= 3.024 V during a short. In the ribbon order only conductors 8 and 10 sat next to GND. D-66 reordered the pads without re-running that adjacency rule; D-66 and PREORDER_REVIEW contain no short analysis for the new grids. Estimate for a pad 5-9 bridge with CHG_ARM HIGH: the U106 output sources about 17 mA through R130 (100 ohm plus about 20 ohm LVC output) from the CR rail fed by R175 68 ohm. V_rail = 3.18/(1+68/120) is about 2.0 V, against the 3.024 V criterion. A pad 1-3 bridge gives 3.3*200/268 = 2.46 V. On the EIB grid the GND_J pads 3 and 9 sit next to 3V3 (pad 2, R177 200 ohm), ILIM_REL_B (4.99 k) and RFC_OFF_J (4.99 k), so the EIB grid has only the 3V3 case.
- **Impact:** A solder bridge on the hand-soldered W5 pads would brown the CR logic rail down to about 2 V and push the LVC output past its recommended current. The direct effect is safe-direction (/CE HIGH or release LOW). However, the comparators and permit gate would then run far below the rail the design analysed, and nothing in the record covers that state.
- **Recommended fix:** Either re-order the CR J101 grid so that GND pads 3 and 9 neighbour only the 4.99 k nets (pads 8 and 10) or STAT, or raise R130 and R131A into the 3.16-10.5 k window that H.24(d) already found feasible, if the CE_DRV and release timing allow it. Then add the 3x3 adjacency check to check_constraints.py and update H.24(d) and J.2 item 70(a).
- **Reviewer confidence:** high
- **Verifier note:** Reproduced the CR J101 pad centres with pcbnew (cr.kicad_pcb, 1.8 mm pads, 1.0 mm drill, 2.54 mm pitch, so the edge gap is 0.74 mm). GND pad 3 at (40.60,50.63) has three orthogonal neighbours: 1 J3V3_1, 2 J3V3_2 and 7 ILIM_REL_A. GND pad 9 at (38.06,55.71) has two: 5 CE_DRV_J and 8 ILIM_REL_B. netlist.py lines 196-197 confirm R130 and R131A are 100 ohm, and lines 202-203 confirm R131B and R132 are 4.99k. R175 is 68 ohm (line 205) and R176/R177 are 200 ohm. For a 5-9 bridge with CHG_ARM HIGH, or a 7-3 bridge with ILIM_REL HIGH, the rail is 3.3*120/188, about 2.1 V. This matches the superseded H.24(a) 100-ohm result of 1.83-2.30 V, which ruling R2 (r9-12) judged worth fixing for pairs 8-9 and 9-10. The D-66 entry (LAYOUT_DECISIONS.md lines 1604-1662) contains no short analysis for the grids. The core finding therefore stands for the new pairs 5-9 and 7-3. Two details are wrong. First, the 1-3 / 2-3 (3V3-GND) case is not new: ribbon conductor 2 already sat next to GND conductor 3, and B.6 (design doc line 2104) and H.20 accept it with the CR rail at 2.30-2.55 V. Second, the claim that 'in the ribbon only conductors 8 and 10 sat next to GND' is false: conductors 2 and 4 neighboured GND 3. The EIB adjacency claim is correct: GND_J 3 neighbours 2 (3V3) and 8 (4.99k), and GND_J 9 neighbours 10 (4.99k). The direct effect is safe-direction (/CE HIGH or ILIM held). However, the T-C-05/T-C-07 short sweeps are still defined on ribbon n/n+1 pairs and need the grid pairs before bring-up, so S2 holds.

- **Same defect as:** EIL-1

#### PWR-0 (S2, CONFIRMED): D103 candidate STPS2H100A does not support the recorded -0.5 to -1 V hard-short OUT excursion, and the acceptance rests on TI guidance for the replaced part

- **Location:** bom_2026-11-13/cr_bom.csv D103 row; mouser/digikey_import.csv D103 (STPS2H100A); docs/charger_bq25798_wiring_design_2026-11-05.md B.4 D103 row (line 1961), H.5 (line 3359), J.1 item 5 (line 3631)
- **Evidence:** TCKE1401NM DS Rev. 3.0 Table 8.1 gives VOUT -0.3 V absolute minimum. Section 12.1 (p.22) says only that an SBD keeps the output from 'dropping significantly below GND'. The design's hard-short case (H.5, H.23(p)) has D103 carrying up to 130 A for less than 1 us. It assumes OUT clamps at -0.5 to -1 V, and J.1 item 5 accepts the excursion 'under SLVSE94G §9.5.1/§11 pending the TI E2E answer'. SLVSE94G is the TPS2663 datasheet, so that basis no longer applies to the fitted Toshiba part. The ordered D103 is STPS2H100A (100 V, 2 A die). The BOM quotes ST DocID6115 Table 4: VF max 0.79 V at 2 A and 0.88 V at 4 A (25 C), an incremental slope of about 45 mOhm. A linear extension of that slope gives about 2.5 V at 40 A and about 6 V at 128 A. IFSM is 75 A (10 ms sine), which meets the >= 75 A criterion (2) with zero margin. Criterion (3), VF at a >= 20 A pulse, and criterion (4), a 130 A non-repetitive peak for <= 1 us, are not answered in the BOM row. I could not open the ST datasheet curves (st.com and farnell timed out), so the high-current VF is an extrapolation.
- **Impact:** In a hard short at the EVM end, U101 VOUT can go several volts below the -0.3 V absolute minimum, against the -0.5 to -1 V figure the risk register relies on. U101 is a single-unit order with no spare (operator ruling), so destroying it in T-C-04 step (2) would stop the build.
- **Recommended fix:** Before T-C-04, read the STPS2H100A Fig. 14 high-level VF curve and its IFSM-versus-pulse-width curve. Re-derive the OUT excursion with the real lead and ESR. Restate J.1 item 5 on the Toshiba basis (DS Table 8.1, §12.1) instead of SLVSE94G and the TI E2E question. If the excursion is several volts, choose a lower-VF, larger-die SMA Schottky that still meets the IR criterion. Alternatively, run the T-C-04 hard-short step last, after the other U101 tests, so that losing the only U101 costs the least test data.
- **Reviewer confidence:** medium
- **Verifier note:** I reproduced each item. TCKE1401NM DS Rev 3.0 Table 8.1 gives VOUT -0.3 V minimum. Section 12.1 (p.22) says only that the SBD keeps the output from 'dropping significantly below GND'. Design doc line 3359 (H.5) assumes OUT at -0.5 to -1 V with D103 carrying up to 130 A, and line 3631-3632 (J.1 item 5) still accepts it 'under SLVSE94G §9.5.1/§11 pending the TI E2E answer'. SLVSE94G is the TPS2663 datasheet, so the acceptance basis is stale since D-67. The cr_bom.csv D103 row quotes DocID6115 VF 0.79 V at 2 A and 0.88 V at 4 A, which gives a 45 mOhm slope, and IFSM 75 A with zero margin. It also states that no VF point at >= 20 A is tabulated. A linear extrapolation gives about 2.7 V at the 45 A typical I_SCP and about 6 V at 128 A. This is an inference; I could not open the ST curve either. Two additions. First, at tens of amps even a larger SMA Schottky will not hold OUT at -0.5 V, so the proposed fix 'choose a lower-VF part' will not by itself restore the -0.3 V rating. The real basis must be either a Toshiba statement or the T-C-04 survival result. Second, H.5 already flags the hard-short figures as TODO(recalc: D-67), but J.1 item 5 does not. Severity S2 stands because U101 is a single unit with no spare.

#### EIB-0 (S2, PARTLY): D-66 wire-pad grids put 3V3 orthogonally next to CE_DRV_J at both ends; one solder bridge defeats the hardware /CE inhibit

- **Location:** eib/eib.kicad_pcb J201 pads 1 (17.46,28.46) and 2 (20.00,31.00) beside pad 5 (17.46,31.00); cr/cr.kicad_pcb J101 pad 1 (38.06,50.63) beside pad 5 (38.06,53.17); LAYOUT_DECISIONS.md D-66 Table D-66
- **Evidence:** Pad positions and nets come from pcbnew dumps of both boards. Each grid is 2.54 mm pitch with 1.8 mm pads, which leaves a 0.74 mm gap. On the EIB, CE_DRV_J (pad 5) sits orthogonally next to both 3V3 pads (1 and 2). On the CR, J3V3_1 (pad 1) sits directly above CE_DRV_J (pad 5). U106A drives CE_DRV LOW through R130 100 ohm (driver Rout 0-37.5 ohm, per H.21). The bridged node, which is also EIB VCC, sits at 3.227 x (100..137.5)/(200..237.5) = 1.61-1.87 V. Inference: U201 must still work at VCC 1.61 V, which is slightly below the 1.65 V specified minimum. The EIB grid also puts 3V3 (pad 2) next to RFC_OFF_J (pad 10). RFC_OFF_J is driven through R132 4.99 kOhm, so a bridge holds it at about 3.1 V. Q202 then turns off and an FC window loses the FC clamp (regen clamp 1.328-1.752 A instead of 0.644-0.944 A). Charging is then stopped only by EN_CHG = 0 and the ILIM hold, and the FR-08(b) I2C trip catches it only after the converter charges. Optionally swap the grid positions so that CE_DRV_J has no 3V3 neighbour; that is a copper change for a later round.
- **Reviewer confidence:** high
- **Verifier note:** Adjacency reproduced from a pcbnew dump of both boards. EIB J201 pad 5 CE_DRV_J (17.46,31.00) is orthogonal to pad 1 3V3 (17.46,28.46) and pad 2 3V3 (20.00,31.00). Pad 2 is also next to pad 10 RFC_OFF_J (20.00,33.54). CR J101 pad 1 J3V3_1 (38.06,50.63) sits above pad 5 CE_DRV_J (38.06,53.17). The pitch is 2.54 mm and the pads are 1.8 mm, so the gap is 0.74 mm. gen/netlist.py lines 196, 206-207, 264-269 and 322-324 confirm the rest of the chain: R130 100 ohm, R176 and R177 200 ohm, J201.1 and .2 both on V33, U201 VCC = V33 (pin 8), and 1A fed through R250 1k / R267 47k (0.979 x VCC). U201 1Y = nCE_DRV, so the bridge forces /CE LOW. The node voltage is wrong because the finding holds the CR rail at the 3.227 V no-fault figure (H.20) and ignores the R175 68 ohm sag. With Vt 3.135-3.465 V, I_base 0.35-1.0 mA and Rd 0-37.5 ohm, the CR rail is 2.29-2.68 V and the bridged node (= EIB VCC) is 1.14-1.55 V, not 1.61-1.87 V. U201 therefore runs further below its 1.65 V rated minimum than stated, and the /CE-LOW outcome is probable but not specified (inference). The RFC_OFF bridge is reproduced: about 3.1 V through the R132 4.99k vs 100 ohm divider, which puts U201 3Y LOW and turns Q202 OFF, so the regen clamp (1.328-1.752 A, H.4 line 3355) applies in an FC window. D-66 / r9-19 (design line 434) records the wire list only and carries TODO(update: D-66). The proposed post-solder isolation check is the right remedy. S2 stands.

#### EIB-1 (S2, PARTLY): CR J101 grid puts GND next to ILIM_REL_A (R131A 100 ohm) and CE_DRV_J (R130 100 ohm), which reopens the CR-rail collapse that R131B/R132 4.99k were raised to prevent

### 4.1 VR-1 (S2, CONFIRMED): the "V-MOT reaches 21.2-21.5 V measured" premise is invalid

- **Evidence:** net RGN-V (`references/Scale_Car_Board_20260624.sch` lines 7698, 9096) joins Teensy pin 39, the
  R1-SNT 78.7 kOhm / R2-SNT 10 kOhm divider and the U-SNT TL431 REF pin. At 15.93 V the two resistors carry 183.1 uA
  and 152.2 uA, so 30.9 uA leaves the node, against a TL431BI Iref of 2 uA typical and 4 uA maximum

  0.995) and 0.54-0.59 on ML0226, against a static ratio of 0.835. A knee model (1.22 V, 9.35 kOhm) reproduces both:

- **Origin of the figure:** `docs/reviews/charger_eval_2026-11-05/round2/regen_dynamics.md:11` sets

- **Figures sized against the premise:** in the wiring design document EM14 (line 2254), the threshold stack
  (line 3400), H.5 (line 3358) and r9-20 (line 435), H.7 (line 3374), the regen rule `I_regen > C x (21.35 - V_bus)`

### 4.2 VR-2 (S2 after the chopper repair, S1 on the board as built; PARTLY): U101 OVP on a hard brake

- **Evidence:** the TCKE1401NM OVP band with R102/R103/R104 is 22.19 / 23.19 / 24.41 V rising and 20.50 / 21.63 /
  22.32 V falling (`tcke1401nm.pdf` Table 10.1). The band minimum lies inside the healthy chopper band of 21.44-22.94 V.
  The chopper is not a hard clamp: R-SHUNT 47 ohm sinks 0.47 A at 22.1 V, so a regen surplus above that and the charger
  draw keeps lifting V-MOT until the VESC cut. If the logged peaks were 22-25 V true, a brake that the charger does not
  absorb is likely to cross the OVP band.
- **Correction to the relayed item and to the design record:** the TCKE1401NM OVP appears to be self-releasing, not
  latched. Section 7.5 says FLAG "remains low level until the overvoltage protection is released", and Table 10.3 gives
  an OVP turn-on delay (190 + 14.5 x C_dVdT = 509 us at C101 = 22 nF, then a dV/dT ramp of about 9.6 ms at 21 V). The
  MODE pin (Table 7.3) is described for the overcurrent response and thermal shutdown. The design's MODE row (wiring
  design line 1966) treats every fault as a latch. TODO(verify: Toshiba), because Table 7.3 says "after the abnormal
  state".
- **Consequence:** if OVP releases, the charger loses VBUS for about 10 ms plus the VBUS re-qualification (FR-23
  T_REGN_MAX, 300-400 ms provisional), the rest of the 0.3-0.6 s burst is lost, and the lost charger load lets V-MOT climb
  further; slow OVP cycling is possible. If OVP latches, only an EN cycle (EN is not connected on the CR), VSYS at 0 V
  or VCHG-IN below the 8.61-9.53 V UVLO falling level resets it, so regen is lost until Idle. In both cases FR-30 does
  not fire (pin 38 is upstream of the eFuse), and no rule recovers a latched eFuse in Run (FW-4).
- **With the chopper failed** (the probable state of this board), nothing in hardware bounds V-MOT except the VESC
  regen cut, whose true level is unknown. The 470 uF CAL capacitor and C-D6/C-D7 are 35 V parts; D-MT-EN and D-BC-RG are
  40 V absolute maximum, and the D-BC-RG OVP is 33 V minimum with no maximum (`RT1987_DS-00.pdf` section 10). D107
  (SMAJ33A, VBR 36.7-40.6 V) sits on VCHGIN and acts only while D-BC-RG conducts. This is why the item is S1 for the
  board as built: hard-regen testing before the chopper repair can exceed the 35 V parts.
- **Clamp on VCHG-IN:** the V_rgn session withdrew its suggestion of a clamp on the CR. T-C-04's rule stands (no
  low-VBR TVS on VCHG-IN, because it would become the regen sink below the D-BC-RG OVP). The remedy is the RGN-V repair
  and a current-limited chopper trip test.
- **Recommended fix:** restore the chopper before any hard-regen test; add an OVP recovery measurement (latch or
  release) to T-C-04; re-derive the OVP band against measured peaks.
- **Operator question:** is a charger dropout on hard brakes acceptable, or should the VESC regen voltage limit be set
  below about 22 V, or the OVP band raised toward the BQ25798 recommended VBUS maximum of 24 V?

### 4.3 VR-3 (S2, CONFIRMED): firmware rules that assume a linear 0.85x V_rgn

- **FR-12(a2), the D-MT-EN short check** (dV_rgn > k x dV_bus with k about 0.5, wiring design line 3258), can miss a
  real short. P0 passes at V_bus >= max(V_fc, V_batt) - 1.5 V and P2 ends at the regulated 15.9 V
  (`teensy_controller.ino` 12837-13010). With the H-20 live (about 13 V open circuit), P0 starts at 11.5-13 V, above the
  knee. A shorted D-MT-EN then reads a ratio of 0.51-0.63 for a window from 11 or 12.5 V to 15.9 V, 0.58-0.68 from 10 V and
  0.69-0.79 from 7-8 V. The measured gains (0.48-0.65) overlap k = 0.5. The rule is in the specification only; the
  firmware has no D-MT-EN check yet.
- **Recommended fix for FR-12(a2):** set k to about 0.3 (a faulty board reads 0.48 or more, a repaired board 1.0, a
  healthy open switch about 0), and require dV_bus >= 2 V. Alternatively, regress over the P0 pre-charge ramp, which
  passes below 10 V where the gain is 1.0. A fixed "V_bus < 10 V" window cannot be guaranteed with the fuel cell live.
- **Other affected rules:** chg_src attribution (r4-19, r6-10, H.22), whose baseline fitted at 15.8-16.0 V cannot learn
  the 0.51 slope and errs by about 0.5 V at 17.5 V and 1.7 V at 21 V; the T1 / H1 cross-check (wiring design line 1435),
  where H1 asserts at a true V-MOT of about 17.3 V and V_rgn reads about 14.15 V; the r6-7 / FR-49 over-range rule, which
  pin 39 cannot reach because the node soft-clamps near a reading of 17.7-18.3 V (the 29.3 V over-range figure applies to
  pin 38 only, and `LIMIT_V_RGN_MAX` 28 V cannot fire); and `MOT_HOTPLUG_MARGIN`, which keeps only 0.35-0.6 V of margin.
- **Recommended fix:** use V_chg (pin 38) wherever REGEN is closed, and use V_rgn only for coarse checks until U-SNT is
  repaired.

### 4.4 VR-4 (S3, CONFIRMED): the CR comparator taps are on full-voltage MB nodes

- **The proposed 0.08 ratio** applies to H1+ only: R110 becomes 44.2 kOhm (0.0802, in range to 43.8 V); R112 must be
