# BQ25798EVM charger path, design r8: KiCad capture and layout decision log

## 1. Scope

This log records every placement and routing decision of the KiCad 9 capture of the r8 charger design
(`docs/charger_bq25798_wiring_design_2026-11-05.md`, commit `dc80dc5`). Each decision cites the design row it
satisfies (an A.7, A.8, A.9, B.8 or H.23 row) or names the deviation and the reason for it. Section 9 lists
the proposed design changes, and section 10 lists every open `TODO(verify)` item. The r8 design document is
not edited by this work.

The capture covers two boards: the carrier **CR** (4 layers) and the EVM interface board **EIB** (2 layers).
The design names no other printed circuit board. The bond coupon of the E BOM is a solder-masked FR-4 offcut
from the workshop, the FT1/FT2 feet and the B1R wedge are machined parts, and the EVM plate is a mechanical
plate; none of them is a KiCad project.

## 2. Sources and reproduction

All board data is generated by the scripts in `gen/`. The scripts are the record of every coordinate.

| Routing | `gen/route.py`, `gen/route_cr.py`, `gen/route_eib.py` | placed board | Freerouting 2.5.0 result with pours |

## 3. Coordinate frame and pin identity

**D-01. Frame.** Every design coordinate is an Eagle board coordinate, top view, origin at the MB origin, +y
up (A.2 r6). The CR capture uses KiCad X = x and KiCad Y = 100 - y, so the CR lies at KiCad Y 24.00-65.86 mm.
The EIB uses its own frame (origin at its lower-left corner, +y up) with KiCad Y = 50 - y. The y inversion is
done once, in `pcbbuild.Frame`; no coordinate is typed in the KiCad frame.

**D-02. Pin identity (A.2, A.8, T-A-06 step 0).** The 22 CHG holes form one footprint
(`Ag105_CHG_Carrier_22pin_P2.54mm`) whose pad numbers are the Ag105 pin numbers. Pin 1 is at (45.998, 39.084);
pins 1-10 run up the x 45.998 column and pins 11-22 run down the x 20.998 column. `check_constraints.py`
compares all 22 pad centres with the A.8 table: the maximum error is 0.0000 mm. Pads 1 and 11 are square, and
the silkscreen carries "1" and "11" on both sides. The only chamfer of the outline is at the tab Y corner
(17.70, 76.00) (A.7 r7), so a y-mirrored capture would move the chamfer to a low-y corner.

## 4. Libraries and symbols

**D-03. Project-local libraries and courtyards.** Each project has its own symbol library
(`charger_r8_<board>.kicad_sym`) and footprint library (`charger_r8_<board>.pretty`), listed in the project
`sym-lib-table` and `fp-lib-table` with `${KIPRJMOD}` paths. The symbols are generated from the B.8 pin tables,
so every pin number is the B.8 number.

The A.8 regions were sized from courtyards of "IPC-7351 nominal land patterns + 0.25 mm". The KiCad stock
courtyards are larger than that basis: the VQFN-24 courtyard is 5.35 mm against a U101 slot of 5.00 x 5.09 mm,
and the seven LVC gates do not fit tab Y. Every footprint on both boards therefore uses the IPC-7351
least-density courtyard excess, 0.10 mm beyond the union of the pads and the body outline (suffix `_CrtYd0.10`).
The pads, fab outlines and 3D models are the stock ones, except as listed below. The CR is hand-assembled with
hot air or reflow (A.8 hand assembly row), so the smaller courtyard excess is a placement clearance, not an
assembly risk.

| Footprint | Change from stock | Source |
|---|---|---|
| U101 TPS26630 | stock `Texas_RGE0024H_..._EP2.7x2.7mm_ThermalVias`; 3 x 3 thermal vias drilled 0.30 mm on a 0.61 mm land, numbered 25; EP solder-mask margin 0 mm (0.149 mm mask web to the pins) | `lib_verification_2026-11-11.md` row 1 (TI RGE0024H); A.8 U101 thermal pad (>= 9 vias, 0.3 mm) |
| SN74LVC DCT gates | pads moved to the TI DCT0008A land: 1.1 x 0.4 mm on a 3.8 mm span | `lib_verification` row 3 |
| SC70-5 gates | stock `Texas_R-PDSO-G5_DCK-5` | `lib_verification` row 5 |
| J101, J201 | Samtec FTSH-107-01-x-DV pattern from the Samtec drawing: pads 2.79 x 0.74 mm, row centres 4.07 mm, body 3.43 x 9.02 mm; J101 has no pad 6 (B.8: 6 omitted, key) | `lib_verification` section 4 |
| CHG header, W1-W4, W6/W7, F01-F24, E1-E8 | written from A.8 / A.9 geometry | A.8 hole and pad tables; A.9 E-pad row |

**D-04. Pin typing for ERC.** U101 OUT (17, 18) and PGOOD (16) are typed passive, because two power outputs on
one net and an open collector on the flagged GND net are ERC conflicts, not circuit faults. PWR_FLAG symbols
mark GND, 3V3 and VCHGIN on the CR, and 3V3 and AGND on the EIB, which are supplied from off the board.

**D-05. Net names.** Where B.8 names a net, the net carries that name verbatim. Where B.8 names only a pad, the
net takes a descriptive name: F01 `T1_VMOT`, F03 `T2C`, F04 `BTB`, F05 `RGN_TAP`, F06 `FCC_TAP`, F07 `T6_3V3`,
F08 `T22_BAL`, F09 `BT_CURR`, F10 `KELVIN`, F11 `QUAL_TAP`, F12 `FCCEIL`, F13 `ILIM_SENSE_RB`, F14 `H1_RB`, F15
`H2C_RB`, F16 `BTOK_RB`, F17 `CHG_EFUSE_FLT`, F18 `CHG_IIN`, F20 `CE_SENSE_RB`, F21 `HB_TAP`, F22 `T18_KA`, F23
`T19_FCI`; F02 and F19 are GND; F24 has no net. The ribbon pins take `J3V3_1`, `J3V3_2`, `CE_DRV_J`,
`ILIM_REL_A`, `ILIM_REL_B`, `RFC_OFF_J`. U111/U113 timing nodes are `N_CX11x` (pin 6) and `N_RC11x` (pin 7).

**D-06. R106 footprint omitted (CR).** B.8 keeps a DNP R106 footprint between `CHG_EFUSE_FLT` and 3V3. The r8
rule of A.8 (R106s placement) requires that the F17 pad is the only exposed copper of `CHG_EFUSE_FLT` apart
from the R106s terminal. A DNP R106 footprint would expose two more pads on that net. The r8 rule is the later
one, so the capture omits R106. Section 9 proposes the matching B.8 change.

**D-07. R_KA designator.** The DNP keep-alive resistor R_KA (B.8, B.5) is captured as **R192** with the value
field "680 0.25W (R_KA)", because a KiCad reference must end in a number for annotation.

### 5.2 Comparator band (A.8 footprint low-y band, A.7 thermal band)

**D-11. Comparator positions.** Four TLV3012 comparators (U115, U119, U103, U102) form the band's upper row at
y 40.10, with courtyards inside x 23.15-39.27. U104 sits in the lower row at (34.56, 36.60), next to F09/F10,
so that its divider bottom reaches the Kelvin pad F10 directly (A.8 Analog row). The band holds only five
comparators at x <= 39.53 with KiCad courtyards. **U120 and U121 therefore take the band positions that A.8
makes conditional on the MS4b reading** ("a comparator courtyard at x 39.53-42.43 ... stands only on the MS4b
reading (T-A-08), else it moves into the interior"): U120 at (38.61, 36.60), courtyard to x 40.51, and U121 at
(41.315, 40.10), courtyard to x 43.21. TODO(verify: T-A-08 MS4b reading). If MS4b fails, U120 and U121 move
into the interior, where the free area is smaller than their two courtyards (section 9, change P-03).

**D-12. Band corners (deviation, section 9 P-04).** Two band corners carry parts that A.8 does not list there:
D108 (BAV199 clamp, analog) in the corner left of F08 (x 22.20-25.40, y 34.64-38.62), and the logic parts U105,
U113 and the DNP footprints in the strip below the pin-1 post (x 42.80-48.20, y 34.64-37.85, and x 43.40-44.80
up to y 41.63). The clamp is analog and meets the 10 mm rim rule (courtyard x max 24.92); the logic parts are
driven logic, which A.7 admits anywhere outside the tap field.

### 5.3 Footprint interior, divider strip and tab Y (A.8)

**D-13. Divider strip.** R109-R112, R114 and R115 (0.1 % dividers) lie inside the A.8 divider strip x 23.15-36.50,
y 42.14-45.69, outside the W3 pad exclusion. Every divider courtyard is >= 13 mm from the MB 5V rim.

**D-14. Interior packing.** The interior region is the footprint inset by 0.5 mm, less the band, the J101 region,
the IN and OUT strips, the U101 courtyard and the +x pocket (each + 0.5 mm), the W1-W4 and W6/W7 pads (+ 0.5 mm),
the header pad columns, the divider strip, the B1R wedge (x 23.55-26.05, y 62.54-67.10, H.23(s)) and the 3 x 3 mm
bead spots S1 (35.00, 47.69) and S2 (28.70, 53.42) (H.23(l)). The B1R wedge and the bead spots B3 (35.00, 70.80),
S3 (35.00, 73.10), S1 and S2 are top-side "footprints not allowed" rule areas, so DRC checks them.

Analog interior parts (BAV199 clamps D104-D106, U123) are restricted to courtyard x <= 39.53 (A.7 thermal band,
10 mm rim rule) and to >= 3.0 mm from the U101 loop region (H.23(f): the dividers keep away from the loop-facing
corner). U123 lies 3.97 mm and D104 4.47 mm from the loop region; the band comparators lie 10.59-14.08 mm from it.

**D-15. Raster packer.** Parts without an A.8 coordinate are placed by `gen/packer.py`: a 0.05 mm occupancy
raster, summed-area tables for exact rectangle feasibility, and up to 150 random-restart orderings. The packer
maximises the number of placed parts, then minimises the distance from each part to the centroid of the pads
it connects to, inside the part's allowed region. The KiCad DRC remains the gate.

**D-16. Tab Y top.** Six LVC gates (U106, U107, U110, U114, U116, U118) and U108/U109 sit on the tab Y top
(A.8 tab Y top row: driven logic only). The 2.0 mm FT2 fill-hole disc carries no part and no copper (B.8
Mechanical). The tab Y top books 62.2 % of its area with the KiCad courtyards.

### 5.4 Bottom side (A.7 bottom clearance rule, A.8)

**D-17. Bottom clearance rule as DRC rule areas.** The rule "every CR bottom-side courtyard stays >= 1.0 mm in
plan from every MB layer-1 feature that is not GND" (A.7 r7) is implemented as B.Cu "footprints not allowed"
rule areas. The geometry is the r7 calculation's own MB data (`round4/calc/r7_calc.py`: 852 non-GND layer-1
traces, 22 polygons with their rim width, 80 vias and 19 non-GND CHG pads, plus the MB part boxes), each grown by
1.0 mm. The KiCad DRC therefore fails any bottom part that breaks the rule.

**D-18. Bottom content.** Every passive that A.8 does not move to the top is a bottom part (the r3 bottom set).
Each decoupler sits under the VCC pin of its IC (B.8 Decoupling). Comparator-input, hysteresis, reference and
timing passives (`layout_cr.ANALOG_BOT`) stay off the tab X bottom (A.7 thermal band: no timing capacitor,
reference or comparator part) and below y 66.862, 10 mm from the L-FC body edge (A.8 tab Y row; L-FC body edge
y 76.862 from H.23(b), an ESTIMATE). The tab X bottom carries only R106s and R148, which are 1 % logic series
resistors (A.8 tab X bottom row). R106s sits on the tab X bottom 3.85 mm from the F17 centre (A.8 r8 R106s
placement).

**D-19. Feet.** FT1 (keep-out x 59.220-64.220, y 65.405-68.300 with the RTV lane) and FT2 (keep-out x
25.500-30.500, y 70.750-75.750) are bottom "footprints not allowed" rule areas (A.8 FT1/FT2 rows). Their body
outlines are on the bottom silkscreen with the FT1 long-axis tick and the FT2 fill-hole ring (PM4 (2c)
placement guide). The FT2 fill hole is a 1.0 mm NPTH at (28.00, 73.250) with a 2.0 mm copper keep-out disc on
all four layers, which covers both the top keep-out disc and the >= 0.5 mm pull-back on L2-L4 (B.8 Mechanical).

## 6. CR routing

**D-20. Stack-up and rules.** The CR is 4 layers (A.8 stack-up r7): L1 signals and power pours, L2 (In1.Cu) a
solid GND plane over the whole outline, L3 (In2.Cu) signals and the power straps, L4 (B.Cu) signals and a GND
pour. The board uses the conservative OSH Park / PCBWay 4-layer rules of `tools/pcb/fab_rules`
(`cr.kicad_dru`); the routed tracks are 0.16 mm with 0.16 mm clearance (fab minimum 0.1524 mm), and the vias
are 0.6 / 0.3 mm (TOOLCHAIN: use a 0.3 mm via drill), with some router vias at 0.56 / 0.26 mm. Every via is
tented on both sides (A.8 L4 vias; A.8 r8 R106s rule for the `CHG_EFUSE_FLT` and `N_FLT` vias). A 0.45 mm
router keep-out band runs inside the outline, because Freerouting does not apply the 0.381 mm copper-to-edge rule.

**D-21. VCHGIN (deviation, section 9 P-01).** A.8 asks for VCHGIN pours >= 2.5 mm wide on 2 oz, and for the
VCHG-IN copper from the pad-10 pin to U101 IN/IN_SYS to be <= 5 mm and solder-masked. The L1 corridor from pad 10
to the IN pins passes between the U101 pin-6 row (y 61.68) and the R102 N_UV pad (top edge y 59.58); with the
A.8 positions it is about 1.7 mm wide, and N_UV must cross it. The capture therefore uses an L1 pour (the IN
strip, the pin 1/2/5 contact and the pad-10 bridge, `route_cr.VIN_L1`) in parallel with an L3 strap from the
pad-10 hole to the C102c via cluster and on to C102b (`route_cr.VIN_L3`, 2.8 mm wide). The L3 strap also
implements the A.8 C102b row: the C102b VCHGIN pad joins the pad-10 / C102c pour on L3 with a via cluster at
each end (five vias at C102b, two at C102c and the pad-10 hole). N_UV crosses the pour on L3. The path from
pad 10 to the IN pins is 3.5 mm long.

**D-22. VCHGOUT (deviation, section 9 P-02).** A.8 routes VCHGOUT from OUT 17/18 along the +y edge to C103, D103
and W1 as a >= 2.5 mm L1 pour. The D103 anode pad lies between the cathode and W1, and the L1 corridor between
D103 and W1 is about 1.5 mm. The capture uses an L1 pour from OUT 17/18 over C103 and the D103 cathode
(`route_cr.VOUT_L1`), four vias under the cathode, and parallel L3 and L4 straps (2.8 mm wide) from those vias to
W1 (`route_cr.VOUT_IN`).

**D-23. VCHG and the GND star.** VCHG joins pins 18/19 and W3 with an L1 pour 5.75 x 4.1 mm (A.8 Copper: VCHG
pins 18/19 to W3). GND joins W4 and pins 14/15 with an L1 pour, and W2 with a local L1 pour; L2 (solid) and the
L4 pour join every GND item (A.8 Copper and stack-up). GND stitching vias sit on a 2.5 mm grid where every
layer has clearance (A.8: stitched at <= 5 mm).

**D-24. Signal routing.** The signal nets were routed by Freerouting 2.5.0 (headless, through Specctra DSN/SES)
and completed by the finishing router (`gen/finish_route.py`): a grid A* (0.1 mm) over L1, L3 and L4 with the
copper of other nets inflated by clearance plus half the track width (tracks) or the via radius (vias). GND
connections close with a via to the L2 plane. A connection that has no free path tears up the routed copper of
the nets it would cross (never pads, never pour nets, never 3V3) and reroutes those nets. The power and pour
nets (VCHGIN, VCHGOUT, VCHG, GND) are never torn up. ROUTE_RESULT_CR

## 7. EIB placement and routing (A.9)

**D-25. Outline (deviation, section 9 P-05).** A.9 gives a 36.0 x 24.0 mm outline with two M3 holes at
diagonally opposite corners 3.5 mm in, each with a 7 mm copper-free keep-out, and the E1-E8 row along the long
edge that faces the EVM (A.5: the pad column runs 7 mm off the EVM right edge). The E row spans 27.94 mm between
its outer centres. On the E-row side, one hole keep-out reaches y 7.0, so the row needs 7.0 + 1.1 + 27.94 + 1.29
= 37.33 mm of long edge. The EIB is therefore **24.0 x 38.0 mm**, the smallest length that meets both A.9 rows.
The E column sits at x 2.0 mm (E8 at y 36.04 to E6 at y 8.10); the holes are at (3.5, 3.5) and (20.5, 34.5).

**D-26. E-pad order and isolation.** The E pads follow the A.9 r6 order top to bottom: E8, (empty), E3, E4, E2,
(empty), E7, (empty), E1, (empty), E5, E6, at 2.54 mm pitch. The empty positions carry no copper. The labels
E1-E8 are on the bottom silkscreen beside the pads.

**D-27. E-side parts.** R276 (E1) and R275 (E7) sit at their pads, 3.2 mm centre to centre (A.9 REGN parts row;
H.23(k)). U203 is targeted at E4 and U204 at E6 (A.9 Analog row); Q201-Q203 sit near E2/E3; U201/U202 sit
mid-board on the signal path from J201. J201 is on the opposite long edge at x 20.0 (A.9 J201 row). The packer
keeps a 0.7 mm routing channel between courtyards (the EIB has area to spare).

**D-28. EIB routing and ground.** Signals run on both layers (0.2 mm tracks, 0.2 mm clearance, 0.6/0.3 mm vias).
The AGND pour on the bottom layer is added after routing and joins E8 (A.9 Ground row); R273 links the J201
GND pins (`GND_J`) to AGND. Freerouting completed the EIB in three rounds; the finishing router is not needed
in the final build.

## 9. Proposed design changes

operator ruling.
