### 4.5 D103 commutation loop (R2-CRL-4): RECORDED with an estimate

Method: the /VCHGOUT F.Cu conductor from the D103 cathode pad (35.50, 35.16) to the U101 OUT pins 17/18 (40.70, 34.39)
is treated as a strip over the solid L2 GND plane. The dielectric height is h = 0.1999 mm (job file, FR408HR 2113).
The strip width comes from the filled /VCHGOUT polygon, cut perpendicular to the 5.26 mm path at 0.1 mm steps: minimum
1.47 mm, median 2.11 mm. The inductance per length is mu0 h / w (parallel plate) or the Hammerstad wide-strip form.

| Segment | Estimate |
|---|---|
| /VCHGOUT strip, D103.1 to U101 pins 17/18 | 0.44 nH (Hammerstad) to 0.57 nH (parallel plate) |
| D103.2 (/GND) to the two /GND vias, 1.8 mm over L2 | about 0.18 nH (parallel plate, width about 2.5 mm) |
| two 0.3 mm vias to L2 in parallel, 0.2 L h (ln(4h/d) + 1) | 0.04 nH |
| Board copper, total | about 0.7-0.8 nH |

The D103 SMA package and the U101 package add inductance that no datasheet in the project gives
(TODO(verify: STPS2H100A / MBRA2H100T3G package inductance)). The copper therefore uses about 0.7-0.8 nH of the A.8
2 nH budget. The budget holds if the package inductance is below about 1.2 nH, which is unverified. Sim 1 uses
L_d = 3 nH nominal with a 6 nH sensitivity case (sim/hardshort/RESULTS.md lines 58 and 267-269). The estimate falls
inside that range, so the sim 1 conclusion is not affected. The estimate script is scratch `loop_l.py`.
