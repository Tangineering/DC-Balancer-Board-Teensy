# Datasheet re-check with the local datasheets (2026-11-17)

This check repeats the pinout, rating and land-pattern checks of `BOM_CHECK_2026-11-17.md` and
`FOOTPRINT_CHECK_2026-11-17.md` for every item that earlier rested on a proxy or an unretrieved datasheet, now from
the datasheets added to `references/Datasheets/` on 2026-11-17. It also checks both boards against the OSH Park
hole rules (docs.oshpark.com, read 2026-11-17). An independent verifier tried to refute each finding.

The check found no S1 finding. One S2 finding survived: the CR plated holes whose unused inner pads are removed
clear the inner-layer pours by only 0.0005 mm more than the OSH Park 0.254 mm rule (section 1). The other confirmed
findings are S3. Note that OSH Park does not state whether a drill size in the files is the drilled or the finished
hole; its FAQ implies the finished size, with a tolerance of +/-0.0635 mm maximum.
