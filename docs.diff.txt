The v6 counters describe what the ESTIMATOR did with the edge stream; these describe the stream itself, which makes them the direct before/after acceptance metric for the pending 74HC14 Schmitt bodge and the only signal that separates a DEAD CHANNEL from a DEAD ESTIMATOR offline (A rising with B flat = sensor fault; both rising with `encoder_pos` flat = decode/quadrature fault; both flat = no motion). Until fw v20 they existed only LIVE, on the fw v19 `'L'` stream and the `'S'` dump. **(b) Geometry levels (new firmware feature, not just a log field):** `enc_phase_ewma` (uint16, offset 100), `enc_duty_a_ewma` (102), `enc_duty_b_ewma` (104) - shift-based integer EWMAs (alpha = 1/4, the fw v13 reference-filter idiom), fixed point in **1/256ths of one slot pitch**, computed entirely in the ISRs. Phase is the last ACCEPTED A-rising edge to the next B-rising edge over `encPeriodRefUs` (the sensor pair's MOUNT geometry); the duties are each channel's rise-to-fall high time over the same reference (that channel's OPTICAL/threshold health, independent of the mount). Phase alone cannot separate a mis-mounted sensor from an off-centre logic threshold on a slow phototransistor edge - duty moves for the second and not the first - so together they **replace a scope for verifying the quarter-pitch offset UNDER ROTATION**, the only condition that matters and the one a static probe cannot reproduce. **Expected healthy on this board: phase 0.25 pitch (64), both duties 0.50 (128).** The 90-slot wheel's sensor offset is 43 deg = 10.75 pitches (fractional part 0.75), but the two sensors were **physically swapped** when that wheel was installed, so A leads B forward and the measured A-rise-to-B-rise fraction is the complement, 0.25. **Bench acceptance, first spin:** phase 0.25 +- 0.05, duties 0.50 +- 0.05 each; drift of phase toward 0.00 or 0.50 is the ALIGNED-EDGES failure this catches (at either limit the channels leave quadrature and the `AfirstUp`/`BfirstUp` handshake loses cycles silently - the fw v8 observability lesson). **Gates:** phase is DIRECTION-GATED to `encPeriodDir == +1`, because a mixed-direction average of phi is meaningless (reverse reads `1 - phi`, so averaging 0.25 with 0.75 lands on exactly the fault signature); every sample is PLAUSIBILITY-GATED at `dt < period_ref`, bounding pollution from a spurious B-edge or an unseen fall injecting phi ~ 0.5. Nothing else is filtered - raw-edge visibility is the point. Samples are valid only once the period estimator is seeded (acceptable: the target is geometric and read at cruise). **ISR budget:** two timestamp latches, one subtract, one shift-EWMA per edge; the ONE divide per sample is confined to the rising/falling branches that produce one (~3.3 kHz worst case at the 5 m/s ceiling), and the common A-rising period path gains NO division beyond the fw v17 fractional-pitch ledger's. **"What NOT to change" exception, same class as fw v15/v17:** this is a TAP - the quadrature decode blocks are untouched, no control path reads any of the three, and the estimator's inputs and outputs are unchanged. All three are also printed in the State-98 `'S'` dump as pitch fractions (value/256.0). **(c) DECODER CONTRACT - the record now carries THREE field classes** and mixing them up produces plausible nonsense: **LEVELS** (`enc_period_ref_us` + the three new ones - read directly, NEVER differenced; a 0 means "no measurement yet", not "0.00 pitch"); **RESET-CLEARED CUMULATIVE COUNTERS** (`enc_multi_pitch_count`, `enc_spurious_drop_count` - negative diff = a mid-run `encoderVelReset()`); **BOOT-MONOTONIC CUMULATIVE COUNTERS** (`enc_edge_count_a/b` - never cleared by anything, the only writes in the firmware are the two ISR increments, so a negative diff is a uint32 wrap or an MCU reset). No clear site may be added for the edge counters: the `'L'`/`'S'` consumers rely on the monotonicity. The three levels ARE cleared by `encoderVelReset()`, together with their latch timestamps, because they are normalised by `encPeriodRefUs`, which that reset also clears. **(d) Ring/drain math re-derived at 106 B:** ring = 106 x 1024 = 108544 B (106 KB, still DMAMEM/RAM2); the ~250 ms stall rationale is stated in RECORDS (1024 samples = 1.024 s at 1 kHz) and is therefore unaffected by the record growing; whole-record chunk floor 512/106 = **4 rec = 424 B/tick** against a 106 B/ms fill = **4.0x catch-up** (down from 5.0x at 100 B and 6.0x at 76 B - still four times the fill; a full ring drains in ~0.34 s); 32 MB preallocation covers 33554432 / 106000 = 316 s (**~5.3 min**, down from ~6.1). No buffer grew; `LOG_RING_RECORDS`, `LOG_CHUNK_MAX`, `LOG_PREALLOC_BYTES` unchanged; `LOG_REC_SIZE <= 255` still holds with 149 to spare. `logSampleTick()` stays a **pure copy** - all five new fields are plain aligned volatile reads (atomic on the Cortex-M7, no IRQ masking) in the build-independent diagnostics section. `offsetof` static_asserts pin 92/96/100/102/104 and `sizeof == 106`. **Decoder/tooling lockstep required** (`tools/decode_benchlog.py` + the analysis pipeline) before the next log round; the phase/duty columns want a LEVEL plot, not a rate. **Next bench:** flash (the flash now carries v18 + v19 + v20); read the `'S'` dump's `phase=`/`dutyA=`/`dutyB=` line on a forward hand-spin FIRST and confirm 0.25/0.50/0.50 before trusting any velocity number on the new wheel; then keep one pre-Schmitt run as the "before" edge-rate baseline, so the same fields quantify exactly what the 74HC14 fixed. | *(pending first flash; fw v18/v19 are not yet flashed either, so the next flash carries v18 + v19 + v20)* |
