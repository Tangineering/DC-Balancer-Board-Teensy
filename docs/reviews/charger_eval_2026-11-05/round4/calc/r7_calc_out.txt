======================================================================================================================
H.23(b) plan clearance of every tab edge/corner to MB part boxes and non-GND copper rims within 5 mm
======================================================================================================================
Part box = mb_neighbours.element_records bbox (pads + layers 21/22/39/40/51/52; BRD). Copper rim: a trace by w/2 around its centreline, a polygon by w/2 beyond its vertex path (outline width from the BRD), a via by its pad radius 0.3782 mm (drill 0.35 + 2 x clamp(rvViaOuter 0.25 x drill, rlMinViaOuter 0.2032, rlMaxViaOuter 0.5080); BRD design rules; confirmed by the 0.9596 mm stop-mask flashes = pad + 2 x mlMinStopFrame 0.1016 in GBR, H.23(c2)), an MB CHG header pad (shape="square" in the BRD) by its square of side 1.650 mm (drill + 2 x clamp(rvPadTop x drill, rlMinPadTop, rlMaxPadTop); the pad corners are not rounded in the model).
Clearance = plan distance from the feature (rim) to the nearest free edge of the tab; < 0: the rim reaches over that edge. 'UNDER the tab' = the centreline/box overlaps the tab (content in H.23(c)); 'under the CR r6 part (wholly inside the r7 outline)' = the feature lies under the r6 rectangle beside the tab root, and the distance to the free tab edges is shown for information. When such a feature also runs OUTSIDE the r7 outline, the clearance and the status refer to the part outside the outline (wires clipped exactly against the outline). For part boxes the body clearance (layers 21 + 51 straight outlines, markers excluded) is given too. GND copper is the board-wide pour and is not listed.
