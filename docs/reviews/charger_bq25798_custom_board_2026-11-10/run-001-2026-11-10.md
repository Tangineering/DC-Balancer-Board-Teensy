### Round-1 findings (verbatim)

#### CCB-R1-01 (reviewer A, MAJOR, class adjudication)

- WHERE: docs/charger_bq25798_custom_board_2026-11-10.md:989-994 (§2.3.2 single-fault coverage); 946-957 (§2.2.2 layout rules); 3038-3039 (T-K-05, T-K-06); 1107 (R515)
- ISSUE: Putting U501 on a hand- or PCBA-assembled board makes a solder bridge between adjacent IC pins a credible single fault. On the EVM this risk sat with TI's factory build. §2.3.2 names only one new fault row (a cracked trace) and does not analyse bridges at the 0.4 mm pitch. The pin order is from SLUSDV2C Fig 5-1. (a) ILIM_HIZ (17) sits next to BATP (18). BATP is VBAT through R515 100 Ohm, and the ILIM_HIZ load is R523||R456 = 39.9 kOhm, so a bridge puts about 0.997 x VBAT = 7.4-8.4 V on ILIM_HIZ whenever Q401 is not biased (REGN off). The ILIM_HIZ absolute maximum is 6 V (DS §6.1). With REGN on and the pin held, Q401 sinks 7.4-8.4 V / 100 Ohm = 74-84 mA, and the 0603 R515 dissipates 0.55-0.71 W, so it opens. On release, the 100 Ohm source overrides the R457 59 kOhm FC leg, so the hardware FC clamp (0.65-0.95 A) is lost and the pin goes above its absolute maximum. (b) ILIM_HIZ (17) next to TS (16): the TS source is R518 against R524||R525, about 3.7 kOhm at about 0.59 x REGN. The released level becomes about 2.6 V, so the ILIM clamp is about 2.0 A and the FC clamp is lost. Only the eFuse I_OL (2.05-2.41 A) and the firmware IINDPM then bound the input current. (c) STAT (1) next to VBUS (2): VCHGOUT (up to 23.63 V) reaches Teensy pin 6 through R475 1 kOhm, about 20 mA, and R475 dissipates 0.4 W. Teensy pad damage is a hypothesis, not verified. (d) /CE (13) next to SCL (14) and /QON (12): I2C traffic pulses /CE LOW. Detection gaps: T-K-05 step 3 measures ILIM_HIZ to GND, which cannot see a BATP bridge because VCHG is high-Z when unpowered. T-K-06 step 2 then applies BATSIM to pins 18/19 with REGN off, so the first exposure to a 17-18 bridge is an absolute-maximum overstress.
- WHAT CAN GO WRONG: A single assembly defect, or a joint that cracks or whiskers on the vehicle, can destroy U501 (ILIM_HIZ above 6 V). It can also remove the hardware FC current clamp without any detection in the interlock: U403 checks only held against released, not the release level. In addition, it can drive VBUS-level voltage into Teensy pin 6. None of the planned pre-power tests detects the 17-18 bridge before BATSIM is applied.
- FIX: Add to the §2.3.2 single-fault table the safety-relevant pairs of adjacent U501 pins: 16-17, 17-18, 18-19, 12-13, 13-14, 1-2 and 4-5, each with its outcome and its detection. Add a pin-to-pin isolation step to T-K-05 before any source is connected. The step measures the resistance ILIM_HIZ-BATP (TP504 to the R515 pad) above 1 MOhm, ILIM_HIZ-TS, NCE-SCL, NCE-NQON and STAT_IC-VCHGOUT. Make X-ray or AOI of U501 a gate, not only a condition of option P. Consider an FR-30-class firmware check of the released ILIM level against REG19 ICO_ILIM on each release, not only at POR.

#### CCB-R1-02 (reviewer A, MINOR, class quantitative)

- WHERE: docs/charger_bq25798_custom_board_2026-11-10.md:1333 (§2.6.1 lead-in); 1348 (Table 2.8 row 'D-BT-SQ shorted')
- ISSUE: The document states that 'in battery-only mode the REGN LDO stays off ([DS] §7.3.3)'. The D-BT-SQ-short row gives REGN off, ILIM_HIZ 0 V and /CE 0 V. SLUSDV2C §7.3.4.1 turns REGN on from the battery when VBAT > 3.2 V and the TS ADC channel is on (ADC_EN = 1 and TS_ADC_DIS = 0). §7.3.3 describes only the POR default. FR-48(a) ([WD] line 2086) sets ADC_EN in exactly this state, with the pack on VCHG at 7.4-8.4 V, to read REG3B. The FR-50 continuous scan also keeps ADC_EN = 1. REGN therefore comes up, /CE rises to REGN through R472/R472b, Q401 turns on and U404 reports /CE HIGH. The keep-alive option (BAT at about 4.5 V through R392) produces the same result.
- WHAT CAN GO WRONG: The firmware design (FR-23 regnExpected, FR-48, the FR-08(b) gating) and the HIL plant are built on a published pin state that does not occur. The result can be a false trip or a missed diagnosis in the D-BT-SQ-short state, and an unaccounted REGN load on the pack through the shorted D-BT-SQ.
- FIX: Correct line 1333 and the row at 1348. REGN is on whenever ADC_EN = 1 with the TS channel enabled and BAT > 3.2 V. The states are then /CE HIGH (inhibit), ILIM_HIZ held by Q401, and U404 HIGH. Add a keep-alive-fitted row. Make sure that FR-23 and FR-48 do not treat CE_SENSE HIGH in this state as a fault.

#### CCB-R1-03 (reviewer A, MINOR, class quantitative)

- WHERE: docs/charger_bq25798_custom_board_2026-11-10.md:834 (D5); 851, 1094 (L501); 887 (§2.1.3 first bullet)
- ISSUE: The inductor floor 'Isat >= 6.5 A' adds an 18 % margin to the 5.51 A worst-case steady-state peak. However, the converter's protection thresholds lie above that floor: IBUS_OCP 7.0-9.0 A and IBAT_OCP 9.3 A (DS EC p.9 and p.12). The Q1/Q4 cycle-by-cycle limits (Q1_OCP, Q4_OCP in the DS block diagram) are not published. The EVM part has an Isat of 11.1 A. A substitute part that meets only 6.5 A can saturate before any IC current protection acts, for example during a SYS or VCHG short, at start-up into a discharged BAT, or in a transient at the eFuse OVP edge. T-K-08 checks only the steady-state peaks.
- WHAT CAN GO WRONG: A part that complies with the stated rule (6.5 A) can let the inductor current run away in saturation during a fault, before the IC's over-current detection operates, and the power stage can be destroyed.
- FIX: Raise the floor to at least 9.3 A at the maximum temperature (the IBAT_OCP and IBUS_OCP maximum), or to the cycle-by-cycle limit after TI confirms it (add this to the O-4 TI query). Otherwise, fix the EVM part SPM6530T-1R0M120 as the only approved part. Apply the same rule to the 750 kHz 2.2 uH alternate.

#### CCB-R1-04 (reviewer A, MINOR, class adjudication)

- WHERE: docs/charger_bq25798_custom_board_2026-11-10.md:1876 (Table 3.9 'MΩ nodes'); 1200-1205 (U320/U321 dividers)
- ISSUE: The FCC_ON and RGN_ON comparator inputs N_FNP and N_RNP use 453 kOhm / 1.00 MOhm dividers, a Thevenin impedance of about 312 kOhm, with no filter capacitor. N_FOP has C330 and N_UVP has C308; these two nodes have nothing. They drive the permit terms H2c (U308 A) and H1q/H1R (U309 B, U318 1B) directly. Table 3.9 leaves both out of the >= 10 mm high-impedance class, although switch nodes at 1.5 MHz and up to 23.6 V now share the board. N_CSP (1M/1M) is listed but also has no capacitor.
- WHAT CAN GO WRONG: Capacitive coupling from SW1, SW2 or L501 into an unfiltered high-impedance permit input can assert FCC_ON or RGN_ON spuriously. That produces a false permit term while the other terms are already HIGH. This is a hypothesis: TLV3012 response times reduce the risk, and T-K-09 is the only check.
- FIX: Add N_FNP, N_RNP and N_CSP to the Table 3.9 high-impedance class. Add C0G footprints from each of these nodes to quiet GND, sized so that the RC time constant stays well below the FR timing budgets (for example 100 pF gives about 31 us on N_FNP). Fit or leave them DNP according to the T-K-09 result.
