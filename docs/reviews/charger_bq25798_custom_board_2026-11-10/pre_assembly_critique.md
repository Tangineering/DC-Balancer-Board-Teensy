## 2. Checks that passed (primary-source samples)

1. CHG pads: an independent parse of `references/Scale Car Board PCB 20260625.brd` (`crit_brd.py`) gives CHG at (47.498, 34.544) R180, drill 1.1, and pad nets STAT=CHRG-STAT, VIN-=GND, SCL, SDA, VIN+=VCHG-IN, MPPTD=MPPT-DISABLE, THERM=N$34, VBAT-1/2=GND, VBAT+1/2=VCHG, all others unconnected. Positions match sec_mechanics Table 1.1 and sec_schematic §8.2 to 0.001 mm.
2. BQ25798 pin numbers 1-29 in sec_schematic §8.4 match SLUSDV2C Table 5-1 (ds.txt lines 134-340); STAT LOW = charging; VAC1/VAC2 to VBUS and ACDRVx to GND without FETs; 1 nF 50 V SDRV-to-GND in rev C; 6.04 kOhm PROG for 2S / 1.5 MHz.
3. Absolute maxima (page 7 rendered): VBUS/VAC/PMID/SW1 30 V, SW2/SYS 23 V, BAT 20 V, logic pins 6 V, STAT/INT 6 mA; ROC VBUS 24 V, ISW 5 A, TA 85 C. sec_schematic Table 2-2 agrees.
4. REG13 bit 5 = PWM_FREQ, bits 7/6 = EN_ACDRV2/1, bit 0 = EN_IBUS_OCP, so 0x01 / 0x21 are as stated. REG10 reset 85h gives VAC_OVP 26 V.
5. The netlist logic reproduces round-3 B.1: UF = UVB.FCCEIL, W = FCCN.UVB, H1q = H1.RGN_ON.W, X = UF + H1R, Y1 = BT_OK.X.FCI_OK, H2c = FCC_ON.BTB_OFF.Y1, GATE = H1q + H2c, ILIM_PRE = GATE + QQF, CE_DRV = P5.ARM_OK', ILIM_REL = ILIM_PRE.ARM_OK', RFC_OFF = FCCN + H1R. The H1 dividers give (442+10)/56.2 : (487+10)/56.2 = 1.089. No BQ25798 pin, gate input or reference node is left floating or single-node.
6. Safety functions all carried: every permit term, the heartbeat monostable, the QUAL one-shot with the QQF mask, OVN/BQ29200 and ARM_OK', the two-switch ILIM release (Q403 feed, Q401 hold), the FC clamp leg, the BTB_OFF/FCC comparators that support I-9, the eFuse and TVS. Each deleted part (R130-R132, R176/R177, J101, R250/R251/R253/R266, R264/R265, R273, C205) has a lead/ribbon-only justification.
7. Input-side ratings: every capacitor >= 50 V (C302 100 V), D303 >= 40 V, U301 67 V abs max; D307 is a clamp (VBR(min) >= 36 V). The IC pins are 30 V parts protected by the eFuse OVP <= 23.63 V; this is stated.
8. Counts and sums: 112 tests (12/26/17/15/14/19/7/2); power-stage 24 placements; TI silicon USD 38.49 (I 26.65, S 11.84); effort totals 417-666 h and 486-822 h; U501 courtyard to foot circles 1.1 / 0.6 mm; Ag105 Fig 11 values.
9. "11 EVM modifications" = [EVAL] §4.2 M1-M11 (verified in the repo), superseded by EM1-EM25.
