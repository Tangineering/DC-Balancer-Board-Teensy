Section 2. R_KA drop at the battery standby current
  I_BAT 120 uA: drop 0.120 V across 1 kOhm; module battery pins at V_pack - 0.12 V
  I_BAT 420 uA: drop 0.420 V across 1 kOhm; module battery pins at V_pack - 0.42 V
  The battery side of the module is therefore supplied from the pack through R_KA
  whenever the input is absent, in the bonded mode included.
