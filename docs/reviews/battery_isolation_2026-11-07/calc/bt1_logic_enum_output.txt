  warm reset from Idle (FF1 clear, PUMP_OK still H), t < 43-76 ms    vmot=above: N1=X FF1=X EF_EN=X bonded=X chg=0
  warm reset from Idle (FF1 clear, PUMP_OK still H), t < 43-76 ms    vmot=bus  : N1=X FF1=X EF_EN=0 bonded=X chg=0
  warm reset mid-window (FF1 set, PUMP_OK still H), t < 43-76 ms     vmot=above: N1=X FF1=X EF_EN=X bonded=X chg=0
  warm reset mid-window (FF1 set, PUMP_OK still H), t < 43-76 ms     vmot=bus  : N1=X FF1=X EF_EN=0 bonded=X chg=0
  after PUMP_OK falls (t > 76 ms)                                    vmot=above: N1=0 FF1=0 EF_EN=0 bonded=1 chg=0
  after PUMP_OK falls (t > 76 ms)                                    vmot=bus  : N1=0 FF1=0 EF_EN=0 bonded=1 chg=0
  FF1=X means the latch state is not determined by the design: /CLR sits at 1.78-1.80 V and CLK at
  1.50-1.55 V. The release of pin 33 from a driven LOW is itself a slow rising CLK edge. The table 12 row
  'Unpowered, reset, hung: p33 Z (pull-down), FF1 clear' and HR5 'FF1 needs a pin-33 edge' are not met
  until PUMP_OK falls (43-76 ms).
