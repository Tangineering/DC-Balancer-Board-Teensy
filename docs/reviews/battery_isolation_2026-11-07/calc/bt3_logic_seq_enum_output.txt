Section 2: pin 5 (MPPT_DISABLE) and pin 30 (REGEN_ENABLE) have two writers inside a Stage-2 regen window
====================================================================================================
  Design 12.4 item 6 removes REGEN writes at ino:[15308, 15375, 15395, 15524] (all LOW writes). ino:15357 (the only HIGH writer:
  'digitalWrite(REGEN_ENABLE, chargerFeedGated ? LOW : HIGH)') is not listed; with AG105_VBATN_ISOLATED = 1
  (design 12.2 item 3) chargerFeedGated = ag105CommTripLatched only. MPPT_DISABLE writes at ino:15304, 15358,
  15377, 15549-15554 are not addressed by any removal, refusal or host test (12.11 lists only REGEN).

  Reading. With charge_goal 0 (OR-O: charge_goal ignored in Run) the charge_goal branch writes pin 5 LOW every 20 ms;
  with charge_goal > 0.05 the regen branch writes pin 5 LOW every 20 ms and pin 30 HIGH every 20 ms. In both cases the
  OR-4 condition (MPPTD HIGH inside every window, section 9.5 entry step 2) holds only until the first chargingControl()
  tick, 0-20 ms after entry, i.e. before or at the first VOK pulse. With charge_goal > 0.05 pin 30 also stays HIGH after
  the exit bond for the remaining 50 Hz ticks of the brake and is re-raised on every later brake tick in the bonded
  mode; ERR_VCHG_FED evaluates only with pins 30 and 31 commanded LOW (Table 16), so it is blind to this feed.

  Invariant (d) on a commanded exit while the brake is still commanded (I_L hold, 30 s cap, ladder mismatch):
  if ino:15357 survives and no write chokepoint refuses it, a chargingControl() tick falls inside the 2 ms
  T_drain in 80 of 800 exit phases (10 %): pin 30 is re-raised, RGQ = VOK & P30Q re-enables the eFuse, and
  pin 33 then goes LOW onto a powered, converting module (the F10 hardware-trip case on a commanded exit).

Section 2b: a brake whose window is refused (V_chg not yet below 11 V, rate limit, or IC1_STAGE 1)
====================================================================================================
  VCHG-IN 21.47 V -> 11 V after 0.658 s (tau 0.984 s); a brake that starts sooner is refused a window by the
  entry precondition 'V_chg below 11 V' (design 9.5). With the rate limit (20 transitions/min) spent, every
  brake is refused. In Stage 1 (IC1_STAGE 1) no brake opens a window.
  Stage 1, charge_goal 0.5, 500 ms brake + 2.5 s cruise, window refused: pin 30 HIGH, pack bonded, for 3000 of 3000 ms (no LOW writer left: 15308/15524 removed);
     D-BC-RG EN = pin 30 directly (M5 not made): VCHG-IN = V-MOT = VBUS through MOT_PWR, continuously; ERR_VCHG_FED not evaluated (pin 30 commanded HIGH);
     eFuse held off only by FF1.Q L and the SRC term (Stage 1: FCQ L) - the 'input unfed in the bonded mode'
     barrier of rows F11/F13b/F35/F36 and of HR3 is gone for the whole brake.
  Stage 2, charge_goal 0.5, 500 ms brake + 2.5 s cruise, window refused: pin 30 HIGH, pack bonded, for 3000 of 3000 ms (no LOW writer left: 15308/15524 removed);
     D-BC-RG on whenever VOK: VCHG-IN fed from V-MOT at every regen pulse; ERR_VCHG_FED not evaluated (pin 30 commanded HIGH);
     eFuse held off only by FF1.Q L and the SRC term (Stage 1: FCQ L) - the 'input unfed in the bonded mode'
     barrier of rows F11/F13b/F35/F36 and of HR3 is gone for the whole brake.

Section 3: self-test step 1 (design 9.3) at boot and at an Idle entry
====================================================================================================
  boot (setup(), pump not started, 12.3 item 5)                                                             step 1 PASS
  Idle entry from Run (pump toggling in every state, 12.4 item 1; Table 12 Idle and Self-test rows p34 = T) step 1 FAIL (mismatch: ['pump_ok'])

  Reading. Step 1 expects PUMP_OK L. The only pump-stop request in the self-test is in step 2, after step 1, and
  12.4 item 1 keeps pin 34 toggling in every state except CHG_INHIBITED, State-99 phase 3 and self-test stop intervals.
  At every Idle entry from Run the pump is running, PUMP_OK is H, step 1 fails on a healthy board, and section 9.3
  sets the reboot-only ERR_CHG_SELFTEST latch, which refuses every window until a power cycle. The boot self-test
  passes, so the defect appears at the first Run -> Idle transition of every power cycle (and in State 98 -> Idle).
