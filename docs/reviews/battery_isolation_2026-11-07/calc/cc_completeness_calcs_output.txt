Section 2. U_MS positive-control deadline (DD 9.5 hold duties, Table 16 ERR_MOD_SENSE (b))
==============================================================================
  From VOK assert (EF_EN rise) at t_D_ON 8.0 ms:
    V_chg reaches 13.5 V at             9.12 ms
    module node reaches U_MS level at  11.77 -  12.51 ms
    lag from V_chg 13.5 V to pin 8 H:   2.65 -  3.38 ms
  EF_EN H interval 10.0 ms with V_chg >= 13.5 V from 9.12 ms: pin 8 HIGH guaranteed = False  -> rev-6 rule would latch ERR_MOD_SENSE
  EF_EN H interval 11.0 ms with V_chg >= 13.5 V from 9.12 ms: pin 8 HIGH guaranteed = False  -> rev-6 rule would latch ERR_MOD_SENSE
  EF_EN H interval 12.0 ms with V_chg >= 13.5 V from 9.12 ms: pin 8 HIGH guaranteed = False  -> rev-6 rule would latch ERR_MOD_SENSE
  -> keyed on the EF_EN interval, the rev-6 rule false-latches on a VOK interval of 10 ms up to
     about 12.5 ms at typical t_D_ON (later with a slower RT1987; t_D_ON has no maximum).
     Keyed on the second settled V_chg >= 13.5 V read (t0), the worst lag is 3.38 ms; a 10 ms span after t0
     leaves 6.62 ms, and with the 9.76 ms worst iteration at least one pin-8 read falls after t0 + 3.38 ms
     whenever the span is >= 10 ms (two reads at most 9.76 ms apart inside it).
  ERR_VCHG_SENSE (b): V_chg passes 12 V at 9.00 ms after EF_EN rise at t_D_ON 8 ms;
     the 20 ms EF_EN-H window false-latches only if t_D_ON exceeds 19.0 ms (no RT1987 maximum; V2-0).
