Section 2. BQ29200 OUT fly-wire: open-wire behaviour, present vs proposed bias

Proposed (R_OVP replaced by 1 MOhm to 3V3): pull-up current 3.3 uA < 30 uA guaranteed sink at 0.05 V -> OUT low <= about 5.5 mV (gate off)
