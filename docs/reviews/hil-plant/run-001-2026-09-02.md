### F1 — MPPT byte 15 is not the board’s threshold during regen

Severity: major

Where: `docs/HIL_PLANT.md:756-774, 1015, 1032`

Claim under challenge: “**THE THRESHOLD VALUE IS THE BOARD’S** … observation-frame byte 15.”

What can go wrong: In HIL, byte 15 is recomputed from `V_chg` on every settled tick, including REGEN. Regen lifts `V_chg` toward the 18.1 V chopper clamp; the real firmware explicitly excludes that voltage from MPPT threshold management. This invalidates byte-15-based MPPT-manager evidence and already caused the `mppt-tracking` campaign failure.

Evidence: The real manager samples only `fcChargePathIsPowering()` and excludes `REGEN_ENABLE` at `teensy_controller/teensy_controller.ino:11090-11096,11570-11577`. The HIL branch instead recalculates the count unconditionally at `:11449-11467`. The latest campaign records the resulting artifact: count minimum 19 where the assertion requires at least 27, explicitly identifying it as a HIL mirror artifact at `HIL Results/hil_report_20260902_011926/REPORT.md:1360-1361`.

Recommendation: State that byte 15 is a HIL-derived proxy, not a board register witness. Make the mirror update only on `fcChargePathIsPowering()`; retain the last valid count or report unknown during regen, then re-baseline `mppt-tracking`.

### F2 — The claimed chopper-to-VBUS path contradicts the strict-forward model

Severity: major

Where: `docs/HIL_PLANT.md:235-240, 925-930, 1583-1594`

Claim under challenge: “It **does** couple to `V_bus` through a closed `MOT_PWR`” and the 18.1 V clamp causes “~0.03–0.06 V of bus sag.”

What can go wrong: At the documented clamp, `V_MOT ≈ 18.1 V` while normal VBUS is about 16 V. `MOT_PWR` is instantiated strict-forward, so `V_BUS - V_MOT` is far below the required +35 mV and the model stamps no BUS↔MOT link. The stated steady-clamp sag mechanism is therefore unreachable in the implementation. A reported 0.0915 J “bus-sourced” contribution may be a start-of-episode precharge/transient, but cannot be attributed to steady clamp coupling without a link-power trace.

Evidence: `MOT_PWR` is `strict_forward=True` at `tools/hil_electrical.py:1752-1753`; its stamp returns without conductance when `v_in - v_out < RT_V_FWD` at `:1102-1103`. The source comments themselves say this correction prevents reverse regen from being absorbed by VBUS at `:1069-1075`. The chopper comment at `:2031-2039` still asserts the opposite.

Recommendation: Remove the steady-state VBUS-sag claim, separate initial MOT-node precharge from clamped-regen energy, and log/integrate MOT_PWR link power before proposing a charger/clamp co-solve as the fix.

### F3 — The document calls a deliberately non-closing residual an energy balance

Severity: major

Where: `docs/HIL_PLANT.md:841-847, 884-892, 1014`

Claim under challenge: “The regen PATH, the chopper clamp and the energy **BALANCE** are now genuine HIL results.”

What can go wrong: The documented residual places `p_chop` on the source side although it is a dissipation. In braking, the implementation’s signed `p_mot` is negative, so the residual contains approximately `-2*p_chop`; it cannot demonstrate energy closure in any chopper-active interval. Calling that balance a genuine result can publish a diagnostic artifact as physical conservation.

Evidence: The implementation defines `p_bal_w = p_mot + p_chg_loss - (p_fc + p_batt + p_chop)` at `tools/hil_plant_sim.py:1948-1996` and explicitly explains the `-2*p_chop` artifact at `:1889-1893`. The document itself acknowledges it at `docs/HIL_PLANT.md:884-891`, then overclaims at `:1014`.

Recommendation: Label legacy `p_bal_w` as a non-conservation diagnostic and exclude braking intervals from balance plots. Introduce a versioned residual with dissipations consistently on one side; preserve the legacy column only for compatibility.

### F4 — “Open loop” is incorrectly documented as always holding the MDAC ratio

Severity: major

Where: `docs/HIL_PLANT.md:520-531`

Claim under challenge: “In open-loop mode it does **not** write the MDACs: it holds the last converged split.”

What can go wrong: That is only the open-hold submode. A fresh low-current run, a changed in-band setpoint, or an outstanding isolation recovery enters open-feedforward and slews/writes the MDAC. Treating all sub-0.55 A operation as inert gives the wrong offline walk and MPC prediction model. This is not theoretical: the shipped MPC Gate 1 has mean delivered-share error 0.00971 against a 0.005 limit, with 0.25 maximum error, concentrated in `open_feedforward`.

Evidence: Firmware distinguishes HOLD from feedforward and calls `applyShareRatio()` for the latter at `teensy_controller/teensy_controller.ino:10141-10214`. The offline governor model mirrors this at `tools/governor_model.py:807-831`. The MPC record identifies the mechanism and failed gate at `docs/modeling/mpc_design_20260901.md:563-600`. The document’s own scenario table later reports 57.12% `open_feedforward` ticks at `docs/HIL_PLANT.md:543-546`, contradicting its earlier absolute statement. The unreconciled b00-v3 census is 20.6% campaign versus 12.7% walk at `docs/HIL_SCENARIOS.md:819-823`.

Recommendation: Replace the statement with the two-mode semantics, including feedforward’s slew limit and trigger conditions. Mark MPC prediction as failed/unvalidated and show both b00-v3 denominators until reconciled.

### F5 — The DP “same model” claim omits a measured fidelity gap and the regen exclusion

Severity: major

Where: `docs/HIL_PLANT.md:1986, 2010-2021, 2043-2050`

Claim under challenge: “makes the objective and the logged `h2_cum_g` the same model” and presents the DP result as the causal-strategy lower-bound reference.

The first FTP-75 DP execution was 2.15% below the table’s H2 prediction and 4.8% more depleted in SoC. In addition, the DP demand model has no regen term, so a regen-bearing run is compared to a regen-free bound and its apparent deviation is optimistic by an unquantified amount.

Evidence: The measured table/run mismatch is recorded at `HIL Results/hil_report_20260901_151156/HIL_FINDINGS.md:54-58`. The generator states the omitted regen term at `tools/gen_dp_ems_table.py:548`; the governing user manual states the resulting optimism at `docs/HIL_USER_MANUAL.md:1750-1757`. The target only gives a qualitative “realised run will differ” caveat, despite this known bound failure.

Recommendation: Add an era-labelled table/run fidelity table containing the -2.15% and +4.8% figures. Do not call DP a lower bound for regen-bearing scenarios; report it unscored until demand includes regen or the bound is explicitly adjusted. Match the DP objective to the reported dynamic H2 metric, or report a separate DC-consistent metric.

### F6 — The universal 1 kHz adequacy claim is false for the hi-fi model

Severity: major

Where: `docs/HIL_PLANT.md:122-137, 1406-1440`

Claim under challenge: “Every dynamic in the model is far slower than the tick” and “The fastest modelled pole is the 0.94 s bus decay.”

What can go wrong: The hi-fi model contains a 0.77 µs switch/capacitance RC and a 100 µs boost lag. Backward Euler prevents instability but does not make soft-start and event numerics independent of step size. The code says the repaired RT1987 current converges only for substeps no larger than about 125 µs, while the adaptive budget permits `n=1`, i.e. a 1 ms simulated substep. Thus hot-plug/SCP event physics can vary with host load without a quality failure.

Evidence: The document itself gives the 0.77 µs network time constant at `docs/HIL_PLANT.md:1437-1440` and the 100 µs source lag at `:1442-1446`. Adaptive wall-clock selection is implemented at `tools/hil_electrical.py:1840-1874`; the model’s own convergence caveat is at `:1270-1272`. The latest FTP run recorded a substep-throughput minimum of 6859 Hz at `HIL Results/hil_report_20260902_011926/REPORT.md:1123-1129`, but no associated numerical-quality gate.

Recommendation: Scope the 0.94 s statement to the simple engine. Log `n` and simulated `h`, enforce a validated maximum `h` for SCP/soft-start verdicts, and mark a run unfit for hot-plug conclusions when that criterion is exceeded.

### F7 — CV taper is both declared absent and used as a passing HIL result

Recommendation: Describe the branch as a synthetic status/taper stimulus, not simulated Ag105 CV regulation. Limit its verdict to firmware handling of injected FULL/CV status and remove the contradictory “no CV taper/stateful SoC” wording.
